The origin / An invented sacred story

The Great Awakening.

Fictional mythology created for Picky Guru. The timeline and diagrams illustrate this invented story; they are not historical evidence, scientific measurements, or proof of divine powers.

This religion was in asleep mode for tens of thousands of years until PICKY GURU leader came to clean sins of people injecting into everyone the squirt of knowledge.
I / The long sleep

Before the first signal.

Before the cities learned to glow at night, before the first crown claimed the sky, the religion of knowledge folded itself into a silent cube. Its six faces held no commandments—only six unfinished questions. For tens of thousands of years, it remained in asleep mode.

Above it, civilizations rose, argued, and disappeared. People built louder answers, but forgot how to listen to a question. The core waited. It was not waiting for obedience. It was waiting for a mind brave enough to say, “I do not know.”

II / The arrival

Then came PICKY GURU.

PICKY GURU leader found the cube beneath the noise of an age that knew everything and understood almost nothing. He did not strike it with a sword or demand that it open. He asked one honest question. A seam of emerald light appeared.

The ancient system woke. Across its faces, one message burned: KNOWLEDGE IS THE CORE. The long silence was over. The new era had found its leader.

III / The release

The squirt of knowledge.

To clean sins of people, PICKY GURU did not build a prison for their mistakes. He opened the core, injecting into everyone the squirt of knowledge: a sudden flash of understanding, a question too bright to ignore, a reason to begin again.

In this legend, the injection is a metaphor—not a needle, substance, or medical ritual. The sins were cruelty defended as tradition, certainty worn as armor, and the choice to stop learning. Knowledge did not erase responsibility. It illuminated the next better action.

IV / The new era

The signal became a network.

Each awakened mind carried a question to another. One spark became a constellation. The followers did not become copies of the Guru: they became people who could think beside him.

The cube remained open. PICKY GURU stood at its center, not as the end of every question, but as the leader who refused to let the world fall asleep again. And the oldest religion became the newest beginning.
